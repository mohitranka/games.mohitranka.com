Lantern Barge

Three rounds with Tamsin “Tugboat” Rourke on a lantern-lit river barge. Punch and guard with your real fists in front of the webcam, or play with the keyboard (F/J straights, D/K hooks, Space to guard). Hand tracking runs in your browser; video never leaves your device.
